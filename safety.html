Digital Payment Safety

Follow these simple rules to stay safe while using digital payments.

🔐 Never Share Your UPI PIN

Your UPI PIN is private. Never share it with anyone.

📩 Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.

💰 Check Payment Confirmation

Always check that payment is received before giving products.

⚠️ Be Careful of Fake Screenshots

Do not trust only a screenshot. Check your payment app or bank account.

📱 Receiving Money

You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

🔗 Avoid Unknown Links

Do not click suspicious links sent by unknown people.
